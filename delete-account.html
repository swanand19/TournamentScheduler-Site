Delete your account

You can delete your account yourself, in the app. It takes a minute.

In the app

  1. Sign in, then open your profile (your name on the home screen).
  2. Choose Edit account, then Delete account.
  3. Read what will happen, then enter the code we send to your email or number.

What happens

  • Your name, email, mobile number and cricket profile are removed, and every device is signed out.
  • Tournaments you alone own are deleted, for everyone in them. Ones with other owners pass to one of them.
  • You're taken off every other tournament.
  • Your name stays on scorecards of matches you played in, because they belong to the other teams as well. Your account is no longer linked to them.
  • Your email and number are freed. If you sign up again with them, you start with a new, empty account.

If you can't sign in

Write to swanand1908@gmail.com from the email address on your account (or say which mobile number it uses), with "Delete my account" as the subject. We'll confirm it's you and delete it within 30 days.
