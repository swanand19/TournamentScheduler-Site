Privacy policy

What the app keeps about you, who can see it, and how to have it removed.

KickCricket runs football and cricket tournaments: teams, fixtures, live scoring, tables and stats. It has no ads, sells nothing about you, and doesn't track you across other apps or websites.

What it keeps

  • Your account: your name, and the email address and/or mobile number you sign in with. There are no passwords: you sign in with a one-time code.
  • Your cricket profile, if you fill it in (role, batting and bowling style).
  • Pictures you add: your profile photo, and a tournament's logo and banner if you own it. They're made smaller on your device before upload, which also removes where the photo was taken.
  • What you and others enter in tournaments: teams, players' names and shirt numbers, fixtures, and every goal, card, ball and result.
  • People you add. When you add an owner, scorer or player by email or number, it's kept so they can be linked to their place, or sent an invite if they have no account yet.
  • Player pools and auctions: if you join a tournament's player pool, your name is on the pool the organisers see, along with any auction bids and the team you go to.
  • Sign-in records: which devices are signed in (a random device id and the app's version), so you can sign out of all of them.
  • Request logs: each request to the server, with its network address, for 48 hours, to find faults and stop abuse. Sign-in codes are stored only in scrambled form, never as typed.

Who can see it

  • Everyone in a tournament sees its teams, players' names, matches and stats.
  • Only a tournament's owners see the emails and numbers used to add people to it.
  • Your profile photo is seen by people in your tournaments, beside your name and on your profile. It is never on public pages; a tournament's logo and banner are.
  • Every tournament is public: anyone with its link can watch its scores, tables and stats and see the names of its teams and players, without an account. Once its schedule is set, anyone signed in can also find it by name and follow it. Emails, numbers, accounts and profile photos are never shown on public pages. If a tournament, or your name in one, should not be public, write to us (below) and we can hide it.
  • A lasting team's page (a team kept between tournaments) can be seen by anyone signed in, and by anyone with its link: the team's name, place and logo, who runs it, its tournaments and results, and its players' names and roles. Emails, numbers and photos are never shown there.
  • Once you play in a tournament, anyone signed in can open your player profile: your name and career stats. Your email and number are only ever on your own.

Messages it sends

Sign-in codes and invites go to the email or number they're for. Updates about your tournaments go to your inbox in the app, and some also by email. Nothing is sent for marketing.

Services it relies on

  • Microsoft Azure hosts the server and database, in India.
  • Google (Gmail) delivers its emails.
  • Meta (WhatsApp) delivers sign-in codes and invites sent to mobile numbers.

Each receives only what it needs to do that job.

How long it keeps things

  • Your account: until you delete it.
  • Inbox notifications: 90 days. Unanswered invites: 30 days. Request logs: 48 hours. Sign-in codes: 5 minutes.
  • Tournaments: until their owners delete them.

Security

Everything between the app and the server is encrypted twice: by HTTPS, and again inside the app with a key only the server holds.

Children

The app is for people aged 13 and over.

Deleting your account

You can delete your account in the app at any time. How to delete your account explains how, and what happens to your data.

Questions
