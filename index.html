A tournament, start to finish

Everything an organiser usually keeps in a notebook, a group chat and a spreadsheet, in one place.

  1. Before the first match

    • Add teams and players by email or mobile number, or share a team's join code.
    • Fill the teams with a player auction or a live random draw.
    • Draw the groups and get a fair fixture list in seconds.
    • Add a knockout stage: semi-finals, quarter-finals or playoffs.

  2. On match day

    • Score goal by goal or ball by ball from your phone, with undo when you slip.
    • Several pitches at once, each with its own scorer.
    • Hand scoring to someone else without losing a ball.
    • Everyone following sees each goal and wicket within seconds.

  3. After the final whistle

    • Group tables update themselves, with net run rate for cricket.
    • Top scorers, wicket-takers and player of the match.
    • The knockout fills in as results come in, up to the champion.
    • Every player keeps a career profile across tournaments.

Everyone at the ground has a place

Each person sees what they need, and only owners can change the tournament.

Organisers
Set up the tournament, invite scorers and other owners, and decide who can watch.
Scorers
Big buttons for every goal, card, run and wicket. The app knows the rules, so it only offers what's allowed.
Players
Join their team with a code, see their fixtures, and follow their own stats.
Families and fans
Watch the scores live from a public link, no account needed. A big-screen board for the hall shows the auction or draw.

Made for both games

Not one sport bent to fit the other. Each has its own scoring, rules and stats.

Football

Halves, extra time and stoppage time, cards and second yellows, rolling substitutions, and penalty shootouts with sudden death.

Cricket

T20, T10, one-day, the Hundred, Tests and gully cricket. Extras, free hits, follow-ons, super overs and DLS targets when rain comes.
